# From measurements to data and AI

I build data platforms and AI workflows for semiconductor engineering. My background spans hardware and embedded firmware, PCIe PHY validation, SSD systems, and measurement automation. Today, I focus on turning measurement, experiment, and log data into reliable pipelines, useful analysis, and practical automation.

## What I work on

- **Data platforms:** ingestion and distributed processing, data modeling, metadata and lineage, and data quality.
- **Engineering analytics:** feature engineering, prediction, anomaly detection, and statistical reliability analysis.
- **Optimization and automation:** connecting automated measurements with reinforcement learning and Bayesian optimization.
- **LLM agents:** analysis workflows that connect models to data and engineering tools through LangGraph and MCP.

## Background

My path started in medical-device hardware and embedded firmware, then moved into semiconductor systems and high-speed interface validation. Since 2023, my work has centered on data engineering and data science, with a growing focus on LLM-based engineering analysis and automation.

I also mentor developers through SSAFY and enjoy sharing knowledge across electronics, software, and data.
